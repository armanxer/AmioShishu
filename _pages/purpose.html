THE REASON BEHIND IT

I am also a child. So is the person still waiting for a chance.

Talent is everywhere. Access isn’t. Through AmioShishu, I support inclusive education and tailored learning for autistic and underserved children. I collected data from 50 children to inform parent-focused learning aids, coached students in IT and mathematics problem-solving, and supervised educational activities.

Even an Olympiad is part of that purpose. I learn to become better equipped to teach. I research to become better equipped to solve problems. An achievement matters more to me when it becomes something I can pass on.
