AMIOSHISHU · INCLUSIVE EDUCATION

I am also a child. Every learner deserves a place.

AmioShishu is a nonprofit initiative advancing inclusive education and tailored support for autistic and underserved children. Its purpose is to make learning more accessible to children whose needs or circumstances are too often overlooked.

My own learning belongs to that purpose. I study and compete to become better equipped to explain, teach, and solve problems. An achievement matters more to me when it becomes something another person can use.

Learning alongside children

I coached students in IT and mathematics problem-solving and supervised educational activities. This work includes a collaboration with Dipto Shimanto. It connects the ideas behind AmioShishu with direct participation in children’s learning.

Support that extends beyond one session

Parents and teachers need practical ways to continue supporting a child with the time and resources available to them. That need is the starting point for my ongoing adaptive-learning research.
